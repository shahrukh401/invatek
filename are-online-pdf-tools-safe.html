Are online PDF tools safe to use?

PDFs often contain private details. Before you drop a file on a website, it helps to know what happens to it.

Two kinds of PDF websites

Most online PDF sites upload your file to their server, process it there and send it back. The file travels over the internet and sits on someone else's computer, even if only for a short time. Other tools work fully inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device. Invatek is built the second way.

Risks of upload based tools

  • Private papers such as ID cards, bank statements and medical reports pass through a server you do not control.
  • Some sites keep files longer than they say.
  • Free sites may show misleading download buttons or ads.
  • Anyone who breaks into the server could reach stored files.

How to choose a safer tool

  • Read the privacy policy. Look for words such as 'processed in your browser' or 'not uploaded'.
  • Use a site that opens with https and has a clear contact address.
  • Be careful with sites that ask you to install software for a simple job.
  • For highly sensitive files, use tools on your own device or offline.

Simple habits

Remove pages you do not need to share. Add a password with Protect PDF before you send private files. Share the password through a different channel. And delete copies you no longer need from your Downloads folder.

Questions

How can I check whether a site uploads files?

Turn on airplane mode after the page loads. A browser based tool still works. An upload based tool fails.

Does Invatek keep my files?

No. Files stay on your device.

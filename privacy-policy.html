Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Your files

Invatek processes your files inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. We do not receive, store or read your documents or photos.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, phone number or email address through the tools. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the time of a visit, for security and to run the site.

Third-party code

When you convert a scan or photo, the language data for text reading is downloaded from a public server. Your file itself is still read only on your device.

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and software libraries (pdf-lib, JSZip, pdf.js, SheetJS, mammoth, docx and Tesseract.js) from public content networks such as cdnjs and jsDelivr. These providers may receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files.

Advertising

Invatek may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings. Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact page.

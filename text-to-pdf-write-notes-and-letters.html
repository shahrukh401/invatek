How to turn text into a PDF

Sometimes all you have is text from a chat, an email or a note. A PDF makes it shareable and printable.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

Good uses

  • A letter or application written on your phone.
  • Study notes you want to print.
  • A shopping or packing list.
  • Chat or email text you need to save as a record.

Steps

  1. Open Text to PDF.
  2. Type or paste your text. Add a title if you like.
  3. Choose the text size: Small, Normal, Large or Extra large.
  4. Tap Create PDF and save or share the file.

About Hindi and other scripts

English text stays real text in the PDF. Hindi and other scripts are drawn as page pictures so they look right on every phone. This means you cannot select the text inside such a PDF, so keep your original text too.

Layout tips

Press Enter twice to leave a blank line between paragraphs. Use Large text if the PDF will be read on a phone. The file name comes from your title, so choose a short clear one.

Questions

Is there a length limit?

No fixed limit. Longer text just makes more pages.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. The PDF is made in your browser.

How to sign a PDF on your phone

Forms and agreements often come as PDFs. You can fill and sign them directly on your screen.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

What you can add

With Edit PDF you can write text, draw with your finger, add lines, boxes and circles, highlight, cover something with a white box, and place a drawn signature.

Steps to sign

  1. Open Edit PDF and choose the file.
  2. Pick the Signature tool and draw your signature on the pad.
  3. Tap on the page where it should go, then move or resize it.
  4. Use the Text tool for your name and the date.
  5. Tap Save edited PDF.

Good practice

  • Sign slowly with your finger or a stylus so the line is smooth.
  • Use the Bigger and Smaller buttons to match the size of a printed signature.
  • Zoom in on the saved file to check that nothing overlaps the form text.
  • Some offices need a digital certificate. A drawn signature is a picture of your handwriting, so check what the receiver accepts.

Questions

Is my signature stored?

No. It is drawn and placed in your browser only.

Can I change the existing text of the PDF?

No. The tool adds new items on top. It does not rewrite old text.

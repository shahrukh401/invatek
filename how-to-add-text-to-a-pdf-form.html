How to fill a PDF form on your phone

Printing, writing and scanning a form is a lot of work. You can fill it on screen in a few minutes.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

What you need

A PDF form and your phone. Not every PDF has fillable boxes. The Edit PDF tool works on any PDF, because it places text on top of the page where you tap.

Steps

  1. Open Edit PDF and choose the form.
  2. Pick the Text tool and tap where the answer should go.
  3. Type your answer and use Bigger or Smaller to fit the line.
  4. Use Draw or Line for ticks and underlines.
  5. Move to the next page and continue, then tap Save edited PDF.

Tips

  • Zoom the page to place text accurately.
  • Use the White box to cover a mistake or old text before you type new text.
  • Use a plain colour like black or dark blue.
  • Fill all pages before saving, and open the final file to check.

After filling

Add your signature with the Signature tool, then compress the file if the portal has a size limit. If the form contains private details, protect the PDF with a password before sending.

Questions

Can I fill in Hindi?

Yes. Hindi text is added as a picture, so it displays correctly.

Can I edit the original text in the form?

No. The tool adds new items on top.

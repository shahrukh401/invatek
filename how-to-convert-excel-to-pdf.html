How to convert Excel to PDF

A spreadsheet is hard to read on a small screen. A PDF is easy to open, print and send.

Updated 11 November 2026

What the tool does

Each sheet in your workbook becomes a section of the PDF. Wide sheets are spread over several pages so the text stays at a readable size. It reads .xlsx, .xls and .csv files.

Steps

  1. Open Excel to PDF and choose your file.
  2. Tap Convert to PDF.
  3. Save the finished file.

Tips for better results

  • Delete sheets you do not need before converting, so the PDF stays short.
  • Keep headings short. Long text in a cell makes tall rows.
  • Charts and images inside the sheet are not included. Numbers and text are.
  • Check totals after converting, especially if you used formulas.

Questions

Why is my wide sheet split?

So the text does not become tiny. Each part appears on its own page.

Does it keep formulas?

The PDF shows the values. It is not editable like Excel.

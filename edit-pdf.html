About Edit PDF

Use Edit PDF to correct a name, change a date, fill in a form or sign a letter without printing it. Pick Change text, tap a line on the page and type the new words. Your text is placed where the old line was and matches its size and colour as closely as possible.

Other tools are Text, Draw, Line, Box, Circle, Highlight, Whiteout and Signature. You can move and resize anything you add, undo mistakes, and go page by page. Change text works on PDFs that contain real text. It cannot read pictures of text, such as scans.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool and add your PDF. The first page appears.
  2. Choose Change text and tap a highlighted line, or choose Text to add a new line anywhere.
  3. Type your change, then tap outside the box. Use Move to adjust it, and Bigger, Smaller, Delete and Undo as needed.
  4. Move through pages with Previous and Next, then tap Save edited PDF.

Tips

  • Change text covers the old line and puts your text on top, using a standard font. Bold or fancy fonts will look a little different.
  • The old text stays hidden inside the file. Do not use this tool to hide private details.
  • Zoom in on the saved file and check that nothing overlaps.
  • For scanned pages, use Text and Whiteout instead, or convert the scan with PDF to Word.

Questions

Can I change the original text of a PDF?

Yes, for PDFs that contain real text. The tool covers the old line and adds your new text on top. It does not rewrite the file's inner text.

Why can I not tap some lines?

Scans, rotated text and text drawn as pictures cannot be picked. Use the Text tool to write over them.

Is my signature stored?

No. It is drawn and placed in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

How to convert PDF to Word

Need to change something in a PDF? Turning it into a Word file is the easiest way to edit the text.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

Two kinds of PDF

Some PDFs contain real text that the tool can copy directly. Others are scans, which are just pictures of a page. For scans the tool uses OCR, which reads the letters from the picture. OCR works well on clear pages and less well on blurry ones.

Steps

  1. Open PDF to Word and add your PDF or a photo.
  2. Choose the language of the text: English, Hindi or both.
  3. Tap the convert button. Scans can take a minute.
  4. Save the Word file and open it in Word or Google Docs.

Tips

  • Always read the result. Check names, dates and numbers.
  • Rescan in better light if the OCR makes many mistakes.
  • You get the text in reading order. Exact design and columns are not copied.
  • Choose Hindi and English together for pages that mix both.

Questions

Will the layout match?

No. You get the text, not the exact design.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It is read in your browser. The first time, language data is downloaded.

How to convert photos to PDF on your phone

Ten separate photos are awkward to send. One PDF is neat. Here is how to make it in under a minute.

When to use Photos to PDF

Use it for ID proofs, handwritten notes, receipts, certificates, drawings or any set of pictures that should travel together. Each photo gets its own page, in the order you choose.

Steps

  1. Open the Photos to PDF tool on Invatek.
  2. Tap Choose files and pick your JPG, PNG or WebP photos.
  3. Arrange them with the up and down arrows.
  4. Tap Create PDF, then Save file or Share.

Get better looking pages

  • Photograph the page straight from above, in daylight, without shadows.
  • If you want shadows removed and the edges cropped, use Scan to PDF instead. It cleans every page.
  • Large photos create large PDFs. Use Compress PDF if you need to upload the file somewhere with a limit.

Photos or scan?

Photos to PDF keeps your pictures as they are. Scan to PDF improves them by whitening the paper and letting you crop. For official documents, scanning usually looks more professional.

Questions

Which photo types work?

JPG, PNG and WebP.

Will my photos be uploaded?

No. The PDF is made on your device.

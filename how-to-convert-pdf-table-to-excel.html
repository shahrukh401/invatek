How to convert a PDF table to Excel

Copying a table from a PDF by hand is slow. This tool reads the rows and puts them in an Excel file.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

How it works

The tool reads the text on each page, finds words that line up in rows and columns, and writes them into cells. If the PDF is a scan, it first reads the letters from the picture using OCR. You can choose English, Hindi or both.

Steps

  1. Open PDF to Excel and add your PDF or a photo of the table.
  2. Choose the language of the text.
  3. Tap the convert button. Scans can take a minute.
  4. Save the Excel file and open it to check the cells.

Tips for accuracy

  • Use a clear, straight and well lit photo or scan.
  • Simple tables with clear gaps between columns work best.
  • Check numbers carefully, especially 0 and O, 1 and 7.
  • Fix any merged or split cells by hand in Excel.

After converting

Sort, filter and add formulas as normal. If you need the data back as a PDF later, use Excel to PDF.

Questions

Why do some cells look wrong?

OCR can misread blurry or small text. Rescan in better light.

Does it work with Hindi tables?

Yes, choose Hindi or Hindi and English. Results depend on the clarity of the page.

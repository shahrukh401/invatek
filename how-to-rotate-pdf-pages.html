How to rotate PDF pages

A scan that opens sideways is annoying for everyone who receives it. Turn it once and save it correctly.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

Rotate view vs rotate file

Many PDF readers let you turn the view while reading, but the file itself stays the same. The next person still sees it sideways. Rotating inside the tool saves the new direction into the PDF.

Steps

  1. Open Rotate PDF and add your file. A small preview of every page appears.
  2. Tap the arrow under a page to turn it right or left.
  3. Use Turn all right or Turn all left to change every page at once.
  4. Tap Save rotated PDF.

Tips

  • Turn only the pages that need it. Other pages stay untouched.
  • Use Reset if you want to start again.
  • Fix rotation before you merge several files, so the final PDF is correct.
  • If pages are tilted by a few degrees and not by 90, scan again or crop them with Scan to PDF.

Questions

Does rotating lower quality?

No. Pages are turned, not redrawn.

Can I rotate by 180 degrees?

Yes. Turn the page twice.

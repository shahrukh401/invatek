How to merge PDF files on your phone

You do not need a computer or a paid app to join PDFs. Here is the simple way to combine files right in your phone browser.

Why merge PDFs?

Sending five separate files is messy. One PDF is easier to attach, easier to read and harder to lose. People merge PDFs for job applications, college forms, bank loan papers, school assignments and travel documents.

A merged file also keeps the order you choose, so the person reading it sees your pages exactly as you planned.

Steps to merge PDFs

  1. Open the Merge PDF tool on Invatek.
  2. Tap Choose files and select two or more PDFs from your phone.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to put them in the right order. The first file becomes the first pages.
  4. Tap Merge PDFs and wait a moment.
  5. Tap Save file, or Share to send it straight to WhatsApp or email.

Tips for a clean result

  • Fix sideways pages first with the Rotate PDF tool, then merge.
  • Name your files clearly before you pick them, for example 01-resume, 02-marksheet. The list is easier to order.
  • If the final file is too large to upload, run it through the Compress PDF tool.
  • Keep a copy of your original files. Merging creates a new file and never changes the old ones.

Is it safe?

Invatek works inside your browser. Your files are not sent to a server, so private papers such as ID proofs stay on your phone. If a PDF has a password, remove the password in the app that made it before merging.

Questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files can be slow on older phones, so merge in smaller groups if needed.

Will the quality drop?

No. Pages are copied as they are.

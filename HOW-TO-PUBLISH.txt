Invatek upload steps
1. GitHub repo me "Add file" > "Upload files" se is zip ki SAARI files ek saath upload karo (zip khol kar).
2. ".github" folder (static.yml) KABHI delete mat karna, wahi site ko publish karta hai.
3. Purani files apne aap replace ho jayengi. Purani extra files (jaise app.js agar pehle thi) bhi replace hongi.
4. Deploy ke baad Search Console me sitemap.xml dobara submit karo.

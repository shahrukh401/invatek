About Scan to PDF

A phone photo of a paper page usually has grey shadows, a yellow tint and a messy background. This scanner fixes that. Pick Clean colour to keep colours and whiten the paper, Black and white for text only pages, or Original photo if you want no changes.

You can take as many pages as you need, turn any page that came out sideways, crop it to the paper, and move pages up or down before you create the PDF. The result is an A4 PDF, or a PDF in the same shape as your photos.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool and tap Take a photo. Your camera opens. Photograph the first page.
  2. Tap Scan another page for each extra page, or From gallery to use photos you already have.
  3. Tap the scissors icon on a page to crop it. Auto finds the paper edges for you.
  4. Choose the look of the pages and the page size, then tap Create PDF and Save file.

Tips

  • Use daylight and hold the phone straight above the page to avoid shadows.
  • Tap the screen to focus before you shoot, and keep the page flat.
  • Use Black and white for printed text, and Clean colour for forms with stamps or coloured text.
  • Many online forms limit uploads to 1 or 2 MB. Check the file size shown after you create the PDF.

Questions

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is cleaned and turned into a PDF inside your browser, so it never leaves your phone.

Can I scan more than one page?

Yes. Add as many pages as you like and put them in order with the arrows.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes. On a computer the button opens a file picker so you can use photos or screenshots.

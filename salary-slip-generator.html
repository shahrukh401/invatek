About Salary Slip Generator

A salary slip, also called a payslip, shows what an employee earned in a month and what was deducted. Small businesses use it to give staff a proper record, and employees need it for loans, rent and visas.

Add the company, employee, pay month, any number of earnings such as basic and allowances, and deductions such as provident fund. The totals and net pay are calculated for you. This tool helps you format a document. For tax and legal questions, check with your CA or tax adviser.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool and enter the company details.
  2. Fill in the employee name, designation and pay month.
  3. Add earnings and deductions rows with amounts.
  4. Download the PDF or share it.

Tips

  • Add or remove rows to match your own salary structure.
  • Use the Notes box for leave balance or an explanation of a deduction.

Questions

Is a salary slip made here official?

It is a record you create. Employers should follow their own payroll and legal requirements.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign up and no limit.

How to scan documents to PDF with your phone

Your phone camera can replace a scanner. A few simple habits give you a page that looks clean and professional.

Updated 11 November 2026

Before you shoot

  • Put the paper on a flat, plain surface with a darker colour behind it.
  • Use daylight or a bright lamp. Keep your shadow off the page.
  • Hold the phone straight above the page so the edges are not slanted.
  • Tap the screen to focus, then take the photo.

Scan with Invatek

  1. Open Scan to PDF and tap Take a photo.
  2. Tap Scan another page for each extra page.
  3. Use the scissors icon to crop, or Auto to find the paper edges.
  4. Pick Clean colour, Black and white or Original photo.
  5. Choose the page size and tap Create PDF.

Which look is best?

  • Clean colour: keeps stamps, logos and coloured ink while whitening the paper.
  • Black and white: smallest files, best for plain printed text.
  • Original photo: no changes at all.

After scanning

Check each page for cut edges. Rotate pages that came out sideways, and order them before you save. Use Compress PDF if the file is too large for an upload form, and Protect PDF if the document is private.

Questions

Can I scan many pages?

Yes. Add as many as you like and reorder them.

Does it need internet?

Only to load the page the first time. The scan itself happens on your phone.

About PDF to Word

PDF to Word pulls the text out of your PDF and puts it in a .docx file so you can edit it. It also reads scanned pages and photos with OCR, which is useful for printed forms and old documents.

The layout is simplified. You get the text in reading order, which is the part most people want to edit. Choose the language before you start for better reading.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool and add a PDF or a photo.
  2. Choose the language: English, Hindi, or both.
  3. Tap the convert button.
  4. Save the Word file and open it to check.

Tips

  • For a scan, use a clear, straight and well lit photo.
  • Check names and numbers after OCR.
  • If the result opens as a .doc file, your device may open it with another app. Use Word or Google Docs.

Questions

Is the layout the same as the PDF?

No. You get the text in order, not the exact design.

Can it read Hindi?

Yes. Choose Hindi or Hindi and English as the language.

Is my file uploaded?

No. It is read in your browser.

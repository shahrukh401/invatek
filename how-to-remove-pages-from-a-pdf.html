How to remove pages from a PDF

A blank last page or a page with private details can be taken out in seconds.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

When to remove pages

  • A scan has a blank page at the end.
  • A cover sheet or advertisement page is not needed.
  • A page contains details you do not want to share.
  • You merged files and one page is repeated.

Steps

  1. Open Remove pages and choose your PDF. The total page count is shown.
  2. Type the pages to delete, such as 2, 4-6.
  3. Tap Remove pages.
  4. Save the new PDF.

Things to know

At least one page must remain. Your original stays unchanged, so if you remove the wrong page you can run the tool again. If you want to keep only a few pages out of many, Split PDF is faster because you type the pages to keep.

Privacy tip

When you hide private details by removing a page, make sure you send the new file and not the original. Check by opening the new PDF before you share it.

Open Remove pages

Questions

Can I undo it?

The original is not changed. Run the tool again on it.

Does quality change?

No. Remaining pages are copied as they are.

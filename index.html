Inverter Battery Basics

An inverter battery stores electrical energy to power your appliances during outages. The two main types are Lead Acid (affordable, reliable) and Lithium (longer life, lightweight, fast charging). Choosing the right capacity (Ah) depends on your total load and backup hours.

For a typical Indian home, a 150Ah lead-acid battery provides 2-3 hours of backup for fans, lights, and a TV. For longer backup or sensitive electronics, consider Lithium batteries with deeper discharge capability.

Lithium vs Lead Acid

Understand the key differences to choose the right battery for your home or office.

Lithium Battery

  • Longer Life: 4000+ cycles (8-10 years)
  • Lightweight: 60% lighter than lead acid
  • Fast Charging: 80% in 1 hour
  • Low Maintenance: No water topping
  • Eco-friendly: No toxic lead

Lead Acid Battery

  • Affordable: Lower upfront cost
  • High Surge: Handles heavy loads well
  • Recyclable: 99% recyclable
  • Reliable: Proven technology
  • Wide Availability: Easy to replace

Which one should you choose?

Top 5 Tips to Extend Battery Life

12 July 2026 By InvaTek

1. Keep terminals clean. 2. Avoid deep discharge below 50%. 3. Ensure proper ventilation. 4. Check water level (for lead acid). 5. Use a smart charger. Following these can add 2-3 years to your battery's life.

Inverter vs Generator – Which is Better?

5 July 2026 By InvaTek

Inverters are silent, emission-free, and cost-effective for short power cuts. Generators are better for long outages and heavy machinery. For most homes, a reliable inverter with a good battery (like Lithium) is the clear winner.

Understanding Battery Capacity (Ah)

28 June 2026 By InvaTek

Ah (Ampere-hour) indicates how long your battery can supply power. For a typical home, a 150Ah battery provides 2-3 hours of backup during an outage. Calculate your load (in watts) to choose the right capacity.

Solar Batteries: What You Need to Know

15 June 2026 By InvaTek

Solar batteries store energy from solar panels. Lithium batteries are preferred for solar due to their high round-trip efficiency and deep discharge capability. InvaTek offers specialised solar batteries for off-grid and hybrid systems.

About Proforma Invoice Maker

A proforma invoice is a preliminary bill sent before goods are supplied or work is done. Customers often need it for advance payment, import paperwork or internal approval. It is not a tax invoice.

The document says Proforma Invoice at the top, lists items with tax, and can show an advance amount. This tool helps you format a document. For tax and legal questions, check with your CA or tax adviser.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool and enter your business and customer details.
  2. Add items with quantity, rate and GST.
  3. Enter an advance amount if you want part payment.
  4. Download the PDF or share it.

Tips

  • Mention bank or UPI details so the customer can pay straight away.
  • Write a validity date so the price is not used after it changes.

Questions

Is a proforma invoice a tax invoice?

No. It is an offer or request for payment. A tax invoice is made when the sale happens.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign up and no limit.

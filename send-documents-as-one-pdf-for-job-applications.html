How to send all documents as one PDF

Many applications ask for several papers. One well ordered PDF is easier for the reader and for you.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

Collect your papers

  • Resume or application letter.
  • Marksheets and degree certificates.
  • Experience letters or offer letters.
  • ID proof, if the employer asks for it.

Get each paper into PDF form

Scan paper documents with Scan to PDF, so the pages are clean and cropped. Convert a Word resume with Word to PDF. Turn loose photos into a PDF with Photos to PDF.

Merge in the right order

  1. Open Merge PDF and choose all your PDFs.
  2. Put the most important one first, usually the resume.
  3. Follow with certificates, then ID proofs.
  4. Tap Merge PDFs and save the file.

Before you send

  • Check the size. Use Compress PDF if the portal has a limit.
  • Open the final file and scroll through every page.
  • Add a password with Protect PDF if you email ID proofs, and share the password separately.
  • Send only what was asked for. Remove extra pages with Remove pages.

Open Merge PDF

Questions

Is one big file better than many?

Follow the instructions of the employer. If they ask for one file, merge. If they ask for separate uploads, do not merge.

Will they see my original files?

No. The merged PDF is a new file.

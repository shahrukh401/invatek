About Invatek

Invatek is a set of free tools for everyday PDF jobs. You can merge PDFs, split a PDF, remove pages, rotate pages, add text and signatures to a PDF, protect a PDF with a password, compress a PDF, turn photos, Word files and Excel files into PDF, and turn PDFs into Word or Excel files.

Your files stay with you

All the work happens inside your own browser, on your phone or computer. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, so we cannot see them or keep them.

Why we made it

Students, shopkeepers and office workers often need to join a few scanned pages, cut out a page, or fix a sideways scan. Most websites ask you to upload private documents to do this. Invatek does it on your device instead.

What it cannot do yet

  • Open password protected PDFs
  • Reduce file size (compress)
  • Read handwriting, or very blurry scans, with full accuracy
  • Keep the exact look of a page when converting PDF to Word

We plan to add more tools over time. Tell us what you need on the contact page.

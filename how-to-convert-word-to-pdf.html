How to convert Word to PDF

Sending a resume or letter as a Word file can change how it looks on someone else's phone. A PDF looks the same everywhere.

All toolsArticlesGuidesAboutPrivacyContact

Updated 11 November 2026

Why send a PDF?

Word files depend on fonts and software. On another phone the layout can shift, and anyone can edit the text by mistake. A PDF freezes the page, so what you see is what they see.

Steps

  1. Open the Word to PDF tool and choose your .docx file.
  2. Tap Convert to PDF.
  3. Wait a few seconds, then save the file.

What to expect

  • Text, headings, lists, tables and pictures are kept.
  • Fonts and layout are simplified, so a very designed document may look a little different.
  • Only .docx files work. Save an old .doc file as .docx first.
  • For Hindi text, use Text to PDF, which draws Hindi correctly.

After converting

Open the PDF and read it once from top to bottom. Check page breaks and tables. If you want to share it privately, add a password with Protect PDF.

Questions

Can I convert many files together?

Convert them one by one, then join them with Merge PDF.

Is my document uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser.
